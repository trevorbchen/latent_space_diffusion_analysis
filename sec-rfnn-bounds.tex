\subsection{Theoretical bounds}
\label{sec:rfnn-bounds}

The four-bulk picture above is established formally in
Appendix~\ref{app:proof-sketch}. The argument decomposes $\Umat$ via
the Hermite expansion of $\tanh$: the linear ($\mu_1$) component of
$\tanh$ produces a rank-$\dlat$ matrix
$\Umat^{\mathrm{lin}} \propto W M_t W^{\top}$ whose nonzero spectrum
inherits the block-diagonal structure of $\Sigmadata$
(\Cref{lem:Ulin}), and the higher-Hermite ($\mu_3, \mu_5, \ldots$)
mass of $\tanh$ collapses onto the diagonal of the empirical kernel
and contributes a $W$-orthogonal rank-$\nsamp$ matrix
$\Umat^{\mathrm{diag}}$ (\Cref{lem:Udiag}). The two ranges are
asymptotically orthogonal in $\R^{\pwidth}$ and the rank-null tail
of size $\pwidth - \dlat - \nsamp$ is the orthogonal complement,
so the four bulks add without overlap. Writing
$\alpha_t^{\,2} \equiv e^{-2t}(s^2/\dint + \sigsig^{\,2}) + \Delta_t$
and $\beta_t^{\,2} \equiv e^{-2t}\signoise^{\,2} + \Delta_t$ for the
signal- and noise-dim-block eigenvalues of $M_t$, and
$\bar\eta_\star = \sum_{k\ge 3}\mu_k^{\,2}\E[(\|x\|^{\,2}/\dlat)^k]$
for the higher-Hermite mass, the formal statement is:

\begin{theorem}[Four-bulk structure, restated from \Cref{thm:fourbulk}]
\label{thm:fourbulk-body}
In the proportional limit
$\dlat,\nsamp,\pwidth\to\infty$ with $\psi_p = \pwidth/\dlat > 1+\psi_n$
and $\psi_n = \nsamp/\dlat$ fixed, the spectrum of $\Umat$ splits
almost surely into four contiguous bulks with counts $\dint$,
$\dlat - \dint$, $\nsamp$, $\pwidth - \dlat - \nsamp$ and
leading-order edges $\mu_1^{\,2}\alpha_t^{\,2}/\psi_p$,
$\mu_1^{\,2}\beta_t^{\,2}/\psi_p$, $\bar\eta_\star/\psi_p$, and
$o(1)$ respectively.
\end{theorem}

Bonnaire's isotropic two-bulk picture is recovered in the limit
$\signoise \to \sigsig$: the signal and noise-dim bulks coalesce into
their single population bulk $\rho_2$ at eigenvalue
$\mu_1^{\,2}(e^{-2t}\sigsig^{\,2} + \Delta_t)/\psi_p$, while the
sample bulk recovers their $\rho_1$. Predicted bulk counts match the
empirical $\textsc{B1}\ldots\textsc{B4}$ sizes in
Table~\ref{tab:bulk-sizes} across the full $\dlat$ sweep at the
predicted indices.

\textbf{Buffer corollary.}
The buffer-mechanism prediction follows by combining
\Cref{thm:fourbulk-body} with the per-mode decay
\eqref{eq:rfnn-mode-decay}: modes are absorbed in order of decreasing
$\lambda_i$, so the time to traverse the noise-dim bulk is bounded
below by its width times the slowest noise-dim timescale,
\begin{equation}
  \taumem - \taugen \;\ge\; (\dlat - \dint)\,\cdot\,
    \frac{1}{\lambda_{\min}^{\text{noise-dim}}}
    \;=\; (\dlat - \dint)\,\cdot\,
    \frac{\psi_p}{\mu_1^{\,2}\,\beta_t^{\,2}},
  \label{eq:buffer-bound}
\end{equation}
to leading order. The signal bulk's slowest mode at
$\mu_1^{\,2}\alpha_t^{\,2}/\psi_p$ sets $\taugen$ and is
$\dlat$-independent at fixed $\sigsig$, $s$, $\dint$, so increasing
$\dlat$ pushes back $\taumem$ linearly in the buffer width
$\dlat - \dint$ while leaving $\taugen$ approximately fixed.
This is the formal statement of the noise-dim buffer mechanism.

\textbf{Where the bulk-edge magnitudes come from.}
At $\sigsig \gg \signoise$ the signal-to-noise-dim ratio
$\alpha_t^{\,2}/\beta_t^{\,2}$ is dominated by
$(s^2/\dint + \sigsig^{\,2})/\signoise^{\,2}$ at small $t$ and
collapses to $1$ as $t \to \infty$ (diffusion noise dominates both
blocks). The noise-dim-to-sample ratio
$\mu_1^{\,2}\beta_t^{\,2}/\bar\eta_\star$ depends on the relative
size of the Hermite-1 coefficient of $\tanh$
($\mu_1^{\,2} \approx 0.367$) and the higher-Hermite mass; the
former dominates at our default scales, so the noise-dim edge sits
above the sample edge and the four bulks remain spectrally
resolvable. Marchenko--Pastur deformations of relative width
$O(1/\sqrt{\psi_p})$ around each edge are sub-dominant at our
$\psi_p = 64$ (Appendix~\ref{app:proof-sketch}, Remark on MP
spreading).
