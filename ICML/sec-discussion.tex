\section{Conclusion and limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\textbf{Conclusion.}
\looseness -1
The experiments support a simple mechanistic picture: latent
dimensions beyond the data's effective signal dimension are not
just wasted coordinates. In the RFNN they appear as a distinct
noise-dimension bulk between population and sample-specific modes.
In the trainable synthetic MLP, increasing the latent buffer sharply
reduces memorization under fixed hidden width. In real VAE latents,
the same force competes with VAE fidelity: small bottlenecks do not
reconstruct well enough to memorize in pixel space, intermediate
dimensions both reconstruct and memorize, and very wide latents
again reduce memorization while often hurting FID.

\textbf{Practical implication.}
\looseness -1
The best latent dimension is not the smallest possible code or the
largest possible code. Too small loses reconstruction fidelity; too
large makes diffusion harder. The useful regime is near the point
where VAE quality has saturated but before the diffusion model pays
too much optimization cost. The spectral predictor in
Sec.~\ref{sec:spectral-predictor} is a first attempt to estimate
this regime directly from the latent data spectrum, without running
every diffusion training curve to completion.

\textbf{Limitations.}
\looseness -1
(i)~The RFNN theory is still incomplete: the current manuscript has
a mechanism and empirical eigenspectra, but the closed-form
anisotropic bulk-edge theorem is left as a TODO. (ii)~The synthetic
data manifold is a linear Gaussian mixture, so curved manifolds and
class hierarchies are only tested indirectly through CelebA and
CIFAR-10. (iii)~The real-data memorization metric is pixel-space
nearest-neighbor ratio on a diverse 1k subset; other definitions
may shift the absolute percentages. (iv)~The VAE and diffusion
systems interact: low-dimensional VAEs suppress measured
memorization by blurring reconstructions, while high-dimensional
VAEs can degrade FID through optimization difficulty. Separating
these effects more cleanly is the main next ablation.
